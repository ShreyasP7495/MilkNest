MilkNest — System Design Document (MVP)
1. Overview
MilkNest is a hyperlocal subscription-based daily milk delivery platform for Indian households. It enables customers to subscribe to milk (initially Nandini and Amul), schedule daily deliveries, and manage flexible subscriptions. Orders are fulfilled through centralized storage hubs and delivered via assigned delivery partners.
The system supports mobile and web applications with real-time delivery tracking, payments, notifications, and customer support.

2. Goals
	•	Provide reliable daily milk subscription delivery
	•	Replace manual milk pickup from local stores
	•	Enable flexible subscription management (daily/weekly/monthly)
	•	Support scalable delivery operations via storage hubs
	•	Provide real-time tracking and notifications
	•	Ensure high availability during morning peak hours

8. Key Workflows
8.1 Subscription Creation
	•	User selects milk + quantity + schedule
	•	System validates limit (<=10 packets/day)
	•	Subscription stored
	•	Inventory reserved
	•	Payment initiated

8.2 Daily Order Generation
	•	Cron job runs at midnight
	•	Converts active subscriptions → orders
	•	Assigns delivery slots

8.3 Delivery Flow
	•	Admin assigns delivery partner
	•	Partner picks items from hub
	•	GPS tracking enabled
	•	Delivery marked complete

8.4 Complaint Flow
	•	User raises complaint
	•	Support team reviews
	•	Refund/resolution provided

9. Business Rules
	•	Max 10 packets per household per day
	•	BNPL adds 10% surcharge
	•	Free offers:
	•	50 packets → +2 free
	•	100 packets → +5 free
	•	Subscription can be paused anytime

10. Scalability Design
MVP Stage
	•	Single region deployment
	•	Monolithic backend (modular services)
Scaling Stage
	•	Move to microservices
	•	Add Kafka event streaming
	•	Multi-region delivery hubs
	•	Read replicas for DB

11. Security
	•	OTP authentication
	•	JWT-based session management
	•	HTTPS everywhere
	•	Payment tokenization (Razorpay)
	•	Rate limiting (Redis)

12. Monitoring
	•	AWS CloudWatch logs
	•	Prometheus metrics (future)
	•	Alerts for:
	•	failed deliveries
	•	payment failures
	•	inventory shortages

15. Conclusion
MilkNest is designed as a scalable hyperlocal subscription commerce platform optimized for daily essential delivery. The MVP focuses on reliability, inventory control, and predictable delivery scheduling while maintaining a modular architecture for future expansion.
